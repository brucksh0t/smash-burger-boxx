The Boxx

A red-and-white box on Ten Broeck.

Same address as Hudson Bagels. Same family.

The smashburger started as a weekday lunch special at Hudson Bagels in January 2025, after months of testing the beef blend, the pickle and a small-batch sauce. It grew into its own spot: a walk-up box on the Hudson Bagels lot with picnic tables out front.

“No shortcuts—just a proper smash burger made fresh every single order.”The Smashburger Boxx, on Instagram
